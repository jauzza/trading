# Phase 4 Final Bounded Audit

This audit uses only the preserved licensed 2018–2025 NQ one-minute cache. No 2026 market observation was opened, downloaded, inspected, or tested. All 2018–2025 data has now been inspected, so no result below is a clean holdout.

## Final decision

**C. Simple opening candle with the original stop plus a fixed 15:55-bar-close exit, for prospective paper trading only.**

The rule is:

1. Wait for the 09:30–09:34 New York opening candle to complete.
2. At the 09:35 one-minute bar open, enter one NQ contract in that candle body’s direction.
3. Place the initial stop at the opposite extreme of the completed opening candle.
4. Keep that stop active for the entire trade.
5. Use no profit target.
6. If the stop has not executed, exit at the close of the one-minute bar timestamped 15:55 New York. On an exchange early close, use the final available regular-session bar.

This choice does not validate the strategy. It does not authorize live trading, automated execution, or use of the untouched 2026 holdout. Its rare-winner dependence is the main reason to collect prospective paper evidence.

## 1. End-of-day execution audit

### Exact implementation

- **Initial stop:** unchanged from 4R and 5R. It is the opposite high/low extreme of the completed 09:30–09:34 opening candle and is active beginning with the 09:35 entry bar.
- **Reference entry:** open of the one-minute bar timestamped 09:35:00 America/New_York, rounded to the 0.25 NQ tick.
- **Simulated entry fill:** reference entry plus one adverse tick for a long or minus one adverse tick for a short.
- **Reference forced exit:** close of the one-minute bar timestamped 15:55 New York. Because bars are start-labeled, this is the close of the 15:55 minute. On an early-close session it is the final available RTH bar close.
- **Simulated exit fill:** reference exit minus one adverse tick for a long or plus one adverse tick for a short.
- **Spread:** one NQ tick per round trip, $5.00 for one contract.
- **Slippage:** one tick on entry and one tick on exit, $10.00 total for one contract.
- **Fees:** $5.10 round trip: commission, exchange, clearing, and regulatory assumptions.
- **Baseline all-in cost:** $20.10 per completed round trip.
- **Same-bar handling:** if stop and target are both touched in a 4R/5R bar, the adverse stop is assumed first. The 15:55 variant has no target, so only its stop can terminate the trade intrabar.
- **Gap-through stop:** fills at the adverse bar open when it is beyond the stop.
- **Sessions:** the identical 1,959 accepted NQ sessions dated 2018–2025.
- **Profit target:** none for the 15:55 variant.
- **Stop differences:** none. Stop price, activation, gap logic, tick rounding, slippage, spread, and fees are identical for all three exits.

Phase 3 represented the no-target exit with an unreachable 100R placeholder. Phase 4 implements `target=None`. All 1,959 new trades exactly match the Phase 3 EOD P&L, proving that the placeholder was never reached and did not cause the surprising result.

## 4. Causal delayed-entry audit

The old 09:36 “no chase” scenario is retained only as an **adverse-selection stress**. It deliberately removes trades that immediately move correctly and therefore selects against continuation winners. It is not a neutral missed-trade simulation.

The corrected delay procedure is causal:

1. Observe only completed bars between 09:35 and the delayed entry.
2. Reject the session if the original opposite-candle stop was touched before entry.
3. Enter at the actual delayed one-minute bar open.
4. Keep the original stop.
5. Recalculate stop distance and the 4R target from the delayed reference entry.
6. Apply the same spread, one tick per-side slippage, and NQ fees.

| Delay | Stop-touched rejections | Matched sessions | Delayed net | 09:35 net on same sessions | Delayed expectancy | 09:35 expectancy |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Enter 09:36 | 109 | 1,850 | $91,535.00 | $132,170.00 | 0.0606R | 0.1495R |
| Enter 09:37 | 212 | 1,747 | $49,045.30 | $184,060.30 | 0.0339R | 0.2211R |

The larger matched 09:35 profits are partly a selection effect: sessions stopped before the delayed entry are excluded from both sides. Even after matching, delay materially reduces normalized expectancy. The corrected result is less catastrophic than the adverse-selection “no chase” proxy but still shows that execution timing matters.

## 7. Prospective paper mode

The local application now contains a separate forward-only journal:

- User selects a future activation timestamp.
- Configuration is one NQ contract, 09:35 opening-candle direction, opposite-candle stop, and one chosen exit.
- Once activated, SQLite triggers prevent configuration updates/deletes.
- Journal events are append-only; update and delete triggers reject mutation.
- Pre-activation sessions are rejected.
- Future-dated event records are rejected.
- Eligible completed sessions store intended entry, available market price, spread estimate, simulated fill, stop, exit, paper P&L, and manual deviation.
- Missed signals, rejected signals, data outages, and manual deviations have explicit event types.
- Prospective paper P&L is calculated only from prospective eligible-session events.
- Historical results are never merged.
- There is no broker adapter and no live or automated order execution.

The interface displays four distinct evidence labels: **Historical exploratory**, **Prospective paper**, **Untouched holdout**, and **Live execution disabled**.

## 8. Why choice C

- **Simplicity:** one direction rule, one initial stop, no target, one fixed time exit.
- **Historical economics:** best net profit and PF of the bounded three.
- **Drawdown:** -25.37%, lower than 4R and 5R in this fixed-contract history.
- **Cost sensitivity:** $99.28 estimated all-in break-even, the largest of the three.
- **Walk-forward stability:** selected from prior years before all four 2022–2025 tests; all four test years positive.
- **Manual practicality:** entry and stop must be handled immediately; winners require holding until late afternoon. A bracket stop plus scheduled paper exit is operationally transparent.
- **Primary weakness:** best-trade and best-five dependence are worse than 4R/5R. Prospective paper collection must determine whether real execution captures those rare trend days.

**Recommendation: C, prospective paper trading only. Do not call it validated. Do not use live or automated trading. Do not access 2026.**
